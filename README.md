BeatSphere is a circle music visualizer where music is displayed as colorful bars around a circle. 

Circle Parts:

Upper-Right: Lowest Frequency Range- Bass/Deep Sound

Right: Low Frequency Range- Lower Music Tone

Lower Right: Mid Frequency Range- Instruments/Vocals

Lower Left: Mid Of High Frequency Range- Instruments/Vocals

Upper Left: High Frequency Range- Captures Brighter Sounds


This is not an exact instrument detector. Some sections will overlap. Added playlist, screenshot and full screen. 

Built as a project for Hack Club's Crescent YSWS [You Ship We Ship] Program under the category Visualizer.
